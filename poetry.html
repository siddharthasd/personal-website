Memories

2003

Time heals the deepest of wounds
though memories remain like scars
like the trail of an ant that has walked upon the sands
and left forever at last.

A Eulogy on the Death of a Seeker

2004

The search had begun,
every-where was the destination
and every-way was the road.

His name was the reason,
for the beginning and the end of his woes.

As babble became words
and poets touched the soul,
questions became thorns,
bled thoughts through every pore.

The sweet ignorance of childhood was torn to bits,
by the dogs of rhyme and reason.
Yet the search would not end,
as the yearning drove through his body, like an electric jolt.

A cushion of velvet money made he,
to comfort him on his dreadful journey.
lest be-knownst no merchant would trade,
a single grain for all his money.

Years became eons,
the pain untold.

Learnt of a currency then he,
that would buy all the grain in eternity.

A mystic man spoke to him, of love and divinity.
wisdom has become me, thought he,
and left in search of love and divinity,
only to trip over an earthly beauty,
into the ghastly valley of lust…

The Fear of Death

2005

For long I wept in silent haze
The inevitable fact of death
I could not imagine, I could not face.

My mothers voice, my fathers smile
my brothers rolling laughter
The warm presence of my mother's mother
will all be lost in times race.

I run to the Buddha, plead to the Jesus
peered into Vishnus eternal gaze.

Oh tell me why you've torn me apart
and tortured me with loves grace.

Bound from the Start

2025

I struggle to abandon attachment,
yet I desire renunciation in my heart.
In aloneness I experience fear,
yet in detachment, there is art.

I find grief in separation
Yet solitude brings peace.
I find comfort in sorrow,
While happiness quickly flees.

How comes this sadness into my heart,
While the world is bursting with life?
Why is comfort found in sadness?
And happiness comes with strife

Solitude and separation are not the same
Yet something binds them from the start
Comfort and sorrow are not the same
Yet something binds them at the heart

The Mirror in Me

2025

I look at the mirror to find meaning
I look at the sky and wonder why

I am a speck on a speck
At the mercy of everything but I

I am a miracle of chaos and chance
Yet here I stand gleaming

I'm euphoric at my luck
And horrified that I am dreaming

For how long will I hold
The reflection of my hand

For how long do I wonder
This universe will stand

As My Son Sleeps

2025

We love but once,
till eternity
pries us apart.

My heart aches
to think
I will hold him
only while I last.

Perhaps
eternal separation
is what makes this bond
so deep

perhaps
the body's fragile existence
is what makes sorrow
steep.

If eternity were gifted to me,
would I love
so fiercely?

If immortality were mine,
would sorrow
cut
so clean?

Is it better to have loved
and lost?

Is it better to have lived
and suffered?

I will never know
for certain
for this fear
makes me weak.

It fills my heart
with longing,
as I watch
my son
sleep.

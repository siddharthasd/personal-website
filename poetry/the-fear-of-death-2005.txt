The fear of death (2005)
For long I wept in silent haze
The inevitable fact of death
I could not imagine, I could not face.
 
My mothers voice, my fathers smile
my brothers rolling laughter
The warm presence of my mother’s mother
will all be lost in times race.
 
I run to the Buddha, plead to the Jesus
peered into Vishnus eternal gaze.
 
Oh tell me why you’ve torn me apart
and tortured me with loves grace.

I struggle to abandon attachment,
yet I desire renunciation in my heart.
In aloneness I experience fear,
yet in detachment, there is art.

I find grief in separation
Yet solitude brings peace.
I find comfort in sorrow,
While happiness quickly flees.

How comes this sadness into my heart,
While the world is bursting with life?
Why is comfort found in sadness?
And happiness comes with strife

Solitude and separation are not the same
Yet something binds them from the start
Comfort and sorrow are not the same
Yet something binds them at the heart

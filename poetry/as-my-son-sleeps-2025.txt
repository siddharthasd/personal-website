We love but once,
till eternity
pries us apart.

My heart aches
to think
I will hold him
only while I last.

Perhaps
eternal separation
is what makes this bond
so deep

perhaps
the body’s fragile existence
is what makes sorrow
steep.

If eternity were gifted to me,
would I love
so fiercely?

If immortality were mine,
would sorrow
cut
So clean?

Is it better to have loved
and lost?

Is it better to have lived
and suffered?

I will never know
for certain
for this fear
makes me weak.

It fills my heart
with longing,
as I watch
my son
sleep.
